What I've learned the hard way

GaN is unforgiving in a way silicon is not. At roughly 100 V/ns slew rate, layout decisions that would be harmless on a silicon design become the dominant error term — and the same parasitics that ruin your switching waveform also corrupt the serial link you were using to observe it. Good layout practice and disciplined in-lab debugging aren't polish on top of the design; on these parts they are the design.
