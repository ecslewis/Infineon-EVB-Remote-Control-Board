5000 mAh Portable USB-C PD Battery Pack

A portable pack targeting 40 W USB power delivery, with conversion that adapts to load size, quiescent current low enough to protect shelf life, and protection covering over-current, under-current and temperature.

02

Architecture

The pack splits into a charge path and a delivery path around a shared cell stack. Input charge arrives through a USB-PD fast-charge front end into the battery charger; a fuel gauge and BMS supervise the Li-ion cells; and an adjustable buck/boost stage feeds the output PD controller so the pack can deliver correct voltage regardless of cell state of charge.

A TVS diode on the input handles surge, and an MCU coordinates the charge, gauge and conversion blocks.

Open questions

Thermal behaviour at sustained 40 W is the part I expect to be hardest — the conversion losses and the cell heating land in the same small enclosure, and the over-temperature protection needs to trip on real thermal risk rather than on normal fast-charge operation.
